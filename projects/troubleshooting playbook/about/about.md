# About the Troubleshooting Playbook

This playbook documents the daily troubleshooting drills I complete each morning to strengthen my real-world IT problem-solving skills.

### Why I Practice Daily
- Build fast, accurate troubleshooting instincts  
- Prepare for real helpdesk and Tier 1/2 support roles  
- Reinforce networking, DNS, VPN, AD, and OS concepts  
- Build a public portfolio of real diagnostic workflows  

### Long-Term Goal
Become a strong IT professional who can break down any issue, trace the root cause, and clearly document the solution.
