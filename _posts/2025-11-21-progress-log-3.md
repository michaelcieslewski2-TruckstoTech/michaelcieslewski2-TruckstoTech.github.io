## 🧰 Daily Troubleshooting Drills

I’ve been running **daily troubleshooting drills** and documenting them in my **Troubleshooting Playbook** on my site.

Each morning, I walk through a realistic helpdesk/desktop support scenario step by step—things like:

- Wi-Fi issues and DHCP problems  
- DNS and shared drive issues  
- Outlook getting stuck on “Loading Profile…”  
- “Slow network” complaints that turn out to be failing hardware  
- VPN problems where the tunnel connects but no traffic flows  
- Login problems caused by domain vs local login confusion  
- Intermittent Wi-Fi caused by power-saving settings  

These drills are already helping me think faster and more clearly when I see issues, and they’re turning into a nice portfolio of real-world troubleshooting examples.

---

## 🐧 OverTheWire – Reaching Level 5

I’ve also been spending time on **OverTheWire: Bandit** to build my Linux and command-line skills.

I’ve made it to **Level 5**, working through things like:

- Navigating the filesystem purely from the terminal  
- Reading files with specific names and permissions  
- Using basic tools like `cat`, `ls`, `find`, `grep`, and redirection  
- Thinking more like a problem-solver instead of just memorizing commands  

It's giving me the muscle memory and confidence I’ll need for both real-world work and future certs.

## 🔐 Getting Closer to Security+

Alongside everything else, I’m getting **closer to being ready for the CompTIA Security+ exam**.

Recent focus areas:

- Core security concepts: CIA triad, least privilege, zero trust  
- Network security: VPNs, firewalls, proxies, DMZs, segmentation  
- Threats and attacks: malware types, phishing, injection attacks, logic bombs  
- Logging and monitoring: SIEM, SOAR, indicators of compromise  
- Identity and access management: RBAC, MFA, SSO, federation  

Between the daily drills, OverTheWire practice, home lab work, and hands-on experience from the new job opportunity, Security+ is becoming more and more within reach.
